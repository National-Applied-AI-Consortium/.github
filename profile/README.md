# National Applied AI Consortium

**Building tomorrow's AI workforce, starting today.**

The National Applied AI Consortium (NAAIC) connects academic institutions, industry leaders, and educators nationwide to build a national AI talent pipeline. It equips educators with the tools, curriculum, and industry partnerships to deliver AI education.

NAAIC is led by [Miami Dade College](https://www.mdc.edu), [Houston City College](https://www.hccs.edu), and [Maricopa Community Colleges](https://www.maricopa.edu), and hosted by Miami Dade College.

## About this organization

This organization holds software built for NAAIC, such as tools and automations that support its programs.

Looking for NAAIC's trainings, resources, or events? Start at [naaic.ai](https://www.naaic.ai).
